---
title: Marry Me Salmon
category: Dinner
ingredients:
  - Salmon filets
  - 2 tbsp butter
  - 4 garlic cloves
  - 3 oz sundried tomatoes
  - 1/4 cup white wine
  - 1 cup heavy cream
  - 1/3 cup mozzarella or grated Parmigiano Reggiano
  - Salt and pepper to taste
  - Parsley for garnish
---
1. * Pat dry your salmon filets and season with the spices above.

   * Sear the salmon in a pan for 2-3 minutes, then set aside.

   * In the same pan, add butter, garlic, and chopped sundried tomatoes.

   * Pour in the white wine and let it simmer for a few minutes to cook off the alcohol.

   * Stir in heavy cream and mozzarella until the sauce is creamy.

   * Return the salmon filets to the pan and cook until the internal temperature reaches 145°F.
