---
title: 1-hour Pizza Dough
category: Breads
prep_time: 1 hour 20 minutes
ingredients:
  - 3.5 cups Flour
  - 2 t salt
  - 2 tsp sugar
  - 2 tsp or 1 packet instant dry yeast
  - 1.5 cups warm water
  - 3 Tbsp olive oil
---
1. In a small bowl, combine water, yeast, and sugar. Mix and set aside for 10 minutes until yeast is active indicated by a foamy mixture forming on top of water.
2. In a separate bowl, mix flour and salt. Add yeast and water mixture to bowl, mix until combined. Once combined knead dough by hand for 5 minutes. Cover with half of olive oil. Let dough rest for 20 minutes. After resting, continue to knead dough for an additional 5 minutes. Cover dough with remaining olive oil and cover bowl with plastic wrap.
3. Allow dough to rise in a warm place for 40 minutes or until doubled in size. Or store in refrigerator for up to 12 hours.
