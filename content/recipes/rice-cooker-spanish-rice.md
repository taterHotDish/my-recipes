---
title: Rice Cooker Spanish Rice
category: Sides
servings: 6
ingredients:
  - 2 cups jasmine rice
  - 2.5 cups chicken broth
  - 1 cup pico de gallo
  - 1 packet of taco seasoning
  - 1 teaspoon salt
---
1. Rinse rice under water until water is clear. Drain water from rice as much as possible on final rinse.
2. Combine remaining ingredients in rice cooker and stir.
3. Cook and serve.
