---
title: Chicken and Dumplings - Simple
category: Dinner
ingredients:
  - 2 10.5 oz cans cream of chicken soup
  - 42 oz chicken broth
  - 3 cups chicken meat
  - 2 10 oz cans refrigerated biscuit dough
---
1. In a large saucepan over medium-high heat, add all ingredients, except biscuits, and bring to a simmer.
2. Cut each biscuit into quarters, stir into simmering pot.
3. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer about 10-15 minutes until biscuits are no longer doughy in the center.
4. Serve.
