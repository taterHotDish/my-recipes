---
title: Chicken and Dumplings - Simple
category: Dinner
ingredients:
  - 1 10.5 oz cans cream of chicken soup
  - 32 oz chicken broth
  - 1 cooked chicken breast
  - 1 10 oz can refrigerated biscuit dough
  - 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables
---
1. In a large saucepan over medium-high heat, add all ingredients, except biscuits, and bring to a simmer.
2. Cut each biscuit into quarters, stir into simmering pot.
3. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer about 15 minutes until biscuits are no longer doughy in the center.
4. Serve.
