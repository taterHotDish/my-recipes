---
title: Foccacia
category: Breads
servings: ""
ingredients:
  - 300g Bread Flour
  - 225g Warm Water
  - 200g Ripe Levian
  - 7g Kosher Salt
  - 7g Olive Oil
  - 7g Honey
  - Any Toppings You Like. Kosher Salt Reccommended.
---
1. Combine all but oil and salt in a bowl and mix until a shaggy dough forms.
2. Pour salt and oil over top of dough without mixing it in.
3. Let rest for 30 minutes
4. Stretch and fold the dough for the next 2 hours every 30 minutes. This will help develop gluten and incorporate the salt and olive oil.
5. After the final stretch and fold, oil the cast iron pan, line with parchment paper, and oil again.
6. Place dough in pan and stretch to fit. Let rise for 1-2 hours until doubled in size. I like to do this on top of a preheating oven where its warm.
7. Preheat ovent to 425F.
8. Dimple the dough with your fingers and add any toppings you like. My favorite is roasted garlic cloves and coarse sea salt.
9. Bake for 25 minutes or until desired color is reached.
10. Let cool on a wire rack before slicing.
