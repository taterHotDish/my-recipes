---
title: "Buttermilk Biscuits "
category: Breads
servings: 8
ingredients:
  - "Two cups all purpose flour "
  - "Six tablespoons butter "
  - "1 cup buttermilk "
  - "Hefty pinch of salt "
---
1. Preheat oven to 450F, and generously butter a cast iron pan. I've been using a #6 pan but I'm not confident on what size my mom uses.
2. Cut cold butter into cubes, add flour, salt, and cubed butter into a large bowl. Using two forks or a dough cutter, break down butter into flour until mixture resembles wet sand. Largest butter pieces should be roughly pea sized.
3. Add buttermilk and mix as little as possible, only until the point where there's no longer any dry flour in the mix.
4. Flour a work surface and put down your dough, also flouring the top of the dough. Either pat or roll dough the flat. This is the part that's kind of difficult to explain and might take some trial and error because the desired thickness of your biscuits and the size of the pan you'll be using are the determining factors, but while touching them as little as possible, roll/press flat and punch biscuits out with a 1 and 3/4" biscuit cutter, ring mold, or in my case a 1½ cup measuring cup that I ripped the handle off of.
5. Place cut biscuits in greased skillet and bake until tops are set, at that point remove from oven and brush with melted butter mixed with salt. Return to oven and bake until golden brown on top.
