---
title: Stromboli
category: Dinner
prep_time: 2 hours
cook_time: 30-35 minutes
ingredients:
  - 1 dough ball - https://recipes.vrylox.com/recipes/1-hour-pizza-dough/
  - 1 cup marinara sacue
  - 3 cups mozzarella
  - 1 pound sliced salami
  - 1 pound sliced pepperoni
  - 1 pound sliced ham
  - 1 egg
  - 1/4 cup milk
---
1. Preheat oven to 425F. Grease a cookie sheet.
2. Roll out dough onto cookie sheet into a rectangle.
3. Evenly spread marinara onto dough leaving about an inch on all sides.
4. Layer salami, pepperoni, ham and cheese onto sauce.
5. Roll dough and ingredients up starting with the long side. Pinch seam to seal and place seam-side down. Finish by tucking ends underneath.
6. In a small bowl, whisk egg and milk to make an egg wash. Brush egg wash on the outside of dough.
7. With a sharp knife create shallow slits along the top of dough to allow for venting. Follow up by applying egg wash to slits.
8. The stromboli is ready to bake. Place in oven and reduce oven temperature to 400F. Bake for 30 minutes or until an internal temperature of 165F is reached.
