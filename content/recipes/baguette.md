---
title: Baguette
category: Breads
ingredients:
  - 100g levian
  - 960g flour
  - 738g water
  - 4g honey
  - 22g salt
---
1. Get two mixing bowls. In the first bowl, add all wet ingredients and stir. In the second bowl combine flour and salt. Stir. Let wet ingredients set 20 minutes.
2. Using the handle of a wooden spoon or spatula, slowly start adding flour-salt mixture to wet ingredients. Mix until all ingredients form a shaggy mess. Scrape mixture off of spoon handle and scrape the sides of the bowl down. Let mixture set for 30 minutes.
3. Stretch and fold dough every 30 minutes for 2 hours.
4. Cover and let rise 8 - 10 hours, depending on how warm or cold your kitchen is. Dough will double in size.
5. Place a pan about a 1/4c of water in a pan in the bottom of the oven. Second rack should be mid-oven. Preheat oven to 450 F.
6. Cover a surface with flour and pour out dough onto surface. Cover top of dough with flour.
7. Using a bench scraper or knife, divide dough into four pieces. Dough will be loose. Just gently stretch into long doughy puddles and transfer (quickly and gently) to a cookie sheet. 
8. Immediately put cookie sheet into the oven. Reduce oven temperature to 425 F.
9. Bake for ~50 minutes or until an instant read thermometer measures an internal temperature of 205 F.
10. Allow to cool for at least an hour preferably three. Slice, butter, eat.
