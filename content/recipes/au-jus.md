---
title: Au Jus
category: Sauces
ingredients:
  - 4 tbsp beef drippings
  - 2 tbsp red wine
  - 2 cups beef broth
  - 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauces
  - salt and pepper to taste
---
#### For Jus From Drippings

2. Place medium saucepan on stovetop over high heat. When saucepan is warm, add 4 tablespoons beef drippings, 2 tablespoons red wine, 2 cups low-sodium beef broth, and 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce.
3. Whisk ingredients together until incorporated, then bring mixture to boil. Once mixture begins to boil, reduce heat under saucepan to medium-low. Simmer mixture 5 minutes.
4. After 5 minutes, taste mixture and add salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste. When satisfied with flavor, transfer jus into serving container. Note: if your drippings contained any remnants from the beef, strain the jus through a fine mesh sieve first, so you're left with a smooth liquid.
5. Serve warm with prime rib, French dip sandwiches, roast beef, or other meal as desired.

#### For Jus Without Drippings

1. Replace beef drippings with unsalted butter in step 1 above.
2. Add ½ teaspoon onion powder and ½ teaspoon garlic powder in step 1 above.
