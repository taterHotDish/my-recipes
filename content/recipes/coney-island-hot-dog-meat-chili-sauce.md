---
title: Coney Island Hot Dog Meat Chili Sauce
category: Sauces
ingredients:
  - 1-1/2 pounds ground beef
  - 1 cup of water
  - 1/2 cup finely diced onion
  - 2 cloves garlic
  - 1/3 cup ketchup or tomato sauce
  - 1 Tbsp mustard
  - 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauces
  - 2 tsp brown sugar
  - 2 Tbsp chili powder
  - 1 tsp cumin
  - 1/2 tsp celery salt
  - Pinch of cayenne powder
  - 1/4 tsp black pepper
  - 1/4 tsp salt
---
1. Put all ingredients in cold pan and stir to combine. You want the ground beef to separate into small granules.
2. Turn heat to med high and bring to a simmer.
3. Turn down heat to a low simmer and cook for 1 to 1-1/2 hours.
4. Add water as needed to the consistency that you like.
