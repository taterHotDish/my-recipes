---
title: Instant Pot Chicken Broth
category: Soups
ingredients:
  - 1 chicken carcass
  - 1 carrot
  - 1 ribs celery + leaves
  - 1 yellow onion, unpeeled, cut in half
  - 1 head garlic, unpeeled, cut in half
  - 1 tsp black peppercorns
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 bay leaf
---
1. Place ingredients in 6 qt Instant Pot. Fill with water just below max fill line.
2. Cook on high pressure for 1 hour if chicken carcass was cooked. Add an additional hour on low if raw. 
3. Allow pot to release pressure naturally.
4. Strain ingredients into a large bowl or pot. Allow broth to cool. Skim fat from top.
5. Store in containers in fridge for up to 5 days, freeze for up to twelve months, or can.
6. Allow broth to chill in fridge prior to freezing.
