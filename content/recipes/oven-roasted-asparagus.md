---
title: Oven roasted asparagus
category: Sides
ingredients:
  - 1 bunch of asparagus spears with woody ends removed
  - 3 tbsp olive oil
  - 1/2 tbsp parmesan
  - 1 clove garlic, minced
  - 1/4 tsp sea salt
  - 1/2 tsp black pepper
  - 1/2 lemon
---
1. Preheat oven to 450℉
2. Toss asparagus in olive oil
3. Arrange in a single layer on a baking sheet.
4. Bake 12 to 15 minutes or until ends start to darken.
5. Squeeze lemon over asparagus. Serve immediately.
