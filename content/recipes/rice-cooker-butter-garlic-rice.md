---
title: Rice cooker Butter Garlic Rice
category: Sides
ingredients:
  - 1 cup jasmin rice
  - 1-1/2 cup chicken broth
  - 2 cloves garlic, minced
  - 2 tbsp butter
  - 1/2 tsp salt
---
1. Wash and rinse the rice well.
2. Add the rice, chicken broth, garlic, butter and salt to the rice cooker pot. Stir the ingredients together well.
3. Set the cooker to cook on the white rice cooking function.
4. When done, fluff the rice and serve.
