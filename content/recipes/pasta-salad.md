---
title: Pasta Salad
category: Sides
ingredients:
  - 1 box of Suddenly Pasta Salad cooked and drained (with packets)
  - 2 or more cups of Italian dressing to your preference
  - 8 oz of cubed cheddar cheese
  - 2 pounds of cherry or grape tomatoes halved
  - 7 oz of chopped ham or salami
  - Sprinkle of Italian seasoning
---
1. Mix ingredients in a large tupperware bowl
2. Chill for at least 2 hours and serve.
