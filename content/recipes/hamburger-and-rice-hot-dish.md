---
title: Hamburger and Rice Hot Dish
category: Dinner
prep_time: 10 minutes
cook_time: 50 minutes
ingredients:
  - 1 pound ground beef
  - 1 can (10-3/4 oz) condensed cream of chicken soup, undiluted
  - 1 cup water
  - 1 cup uncokked instant rice
  - Minced fresh parsley (optional)
---

1. In a large skillet, cook beef over medium heat until no longer pink; drain. Stir in the remaining ingredients. Spoon into a 9x13 baking dish.
2. Cover and bake at 325° for 50-60 minutes or until rice is tender.
